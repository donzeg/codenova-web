## 10. ENGAGEMENT MODELS & PRICING

### Project-Based Engagement
- **Best for:** Well-defined projects with clear scope
- **Pricing:** Fixed fee for agreed scope
- **Timeline:** Defined start & end dates
- **Benefit:** Budget certainty & clear deliverables

### Time & Materials
- **Best for:** Exploratory projects, R&D, evolving requirements
- **Pricing:** Hourly or daily rate + expenses
- **Timeline:** Flexible, evolving scope
- **Benefit:** Flexibility, transparent billing, pay for actual work

### Retainer / Support
- **Best for:** Ongoing maintenance, enhancements, support
- **Pricing:** Monthly retainer fee
- **Timeline:** Continuous engagement
- **Benefit:** Dedicated team availability, priority support, cost predictability

### Dedicated Team
- **Best for:** Large projects, long-term engagement
- **Pricing:** Monthly fee per team member
- **Timeline:** Multi-month/year engagements
- **Benefit:** Complete team focused on your project
