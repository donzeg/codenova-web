## Statutory & Mandatory Deductions

### 1. Personal Income Tax (PAYE)
Based on **2026 Nigerian Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS) tax bands**:
- Standard relief: ₦200,000 annual (₦16,667 monthly equivalent)
- Consolidated relief allowance: 1% of gross annual salary OR ₦200,000 (whichever is higher)  
- Progressive tax bands applied to taxable income

**Calculation Formula:** Taxable Income = (Gross Salary × 12) - Consolidated Relief - Standard Relief

### 2. Pension Contributions (Mandatory)
- **Employee Contribution:** 8% of gross salary (deducted from salary)
- **Employer Contribution:** 10% of gross salary (company cost, not deducted from employee)
- **Total Pension Scheme:** 18%
- **Administrator:** Remitted to employee's Pension Fund Administrator (PFA) monthly
- **Requirement:** Employees must provide RSA PIN within 14 days of employment start

### 3. National Health Insurance Scheme (NHIS)
- **Employee Premium:** 5% of gross salary (optional; can be employer-sponsored)
- **Employer Option:** CodeNova provides basic NHIS coverage (employer bears cost)
- **Coverage:** Employee + basic family coverage

### 4. Other Statutory Deductions
- **Tax Identifiable Number (TIN):** Required for all employees for PAYE filing
- **National Identification Number (NIN):** Required for payroll compliance

## Compliance & Regulatory Requirements

### PAYE & Tax Filing
- **Responsible Officer:** HR Manager (Safiya Aminu)
- **Filing Deadline:** Monthly filings to FIRS on or before 21st of following month
- **Required Documents:** Payroll register, TIN list, employee relief certificates
- **Proof:** Maintain copies of FIRS acknowledgment for each filing

### Pension Remittance
- **Frequency:** Monthly (before 25th of each month)
- **Recipient:** Each employee's nominated PFA
- **Documentation:** Proof of remittance must be filed and shared with employees
- **Employee RSA PIN:** All employees must register with PFA; HR tracks PIN confirmation

### NHIS Contribution
- **Frequency:** Monthly
- **Provider:** Registered NHIS provider or private insurance
- **Coverage:** Employee + dependent(s) as per policy
- **Documentation:** Maintain premium payment receipts

### National Identification
- **TIN Registration:** All employees must register with FIRS (processing time ~2 weeks)
- **NIN Verification:** BVN (Bank Verification Number) linked to NIN for payroll processing
- **Employee Responsibility:** Provide valid NIN and TIN before first salary payment

### Record Keeping
- **Payroll Register:** Maintained by HR, signed by authorized signatories
- **Retention Period:** Minimum 6 years per Nigerian Labor Law
- **Access:** Available to employees for verification; provided to auditors/regulators on request

## Annual Leave & Gratuity

- **Annual Leave Entitlement:** 20 working days per employee per annum
- **Leave Payment:** Computed at current salary rate
- **Gratuity:** Per Labor Act — 2 weeks' salary for every year of service (minimum 5 years)
- **Final Settlement:** Upon termination, all accrued leave and gratuity computed and paid

## Notes & Important Information

1. **PAYE Estimates:** PAYE figures shown are estimates. Actual PAYE depends on:
   - Individual relief certificates (Form IT1 or personal relief)
   - Number of dependents claimed
   - Housing loan interest (if applicable)
   - Pension contributions already made
   
2. **Annual Review:** Salary structure reviewed annually at year-end; adjustments effective from January.

3. **Statutory Minimum Wage:** Current (as of Feb 2026) is ₦35,000/month. All salaries meet or exceed this requirement. If statutory minimum increases, all salaries will be adjusted accordingly.

4. **Tax Exemptions:** Certain allowances may be tax-exempt (e.g., leave travel allowance, moving allowance) — to be documented separately if applicable.

5. **Dispute Resolution:** Any payroll queries or discrepancies must be raised with HR within 14 days of salary payment.

6. **Confidentiality:** Salary details are confidential. Unauthorized disclosure of employee salaries is a breach of employment terms.
