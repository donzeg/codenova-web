## Statutory Deductions (Per Employee)

### 1. Pension (Mandatory)
- **Employee Contribution:** 8% of gross (deducted from salary)
- **Employer Contribution:** 10% of gross (company pays; not deducted from employee)
- **Administrator:** Remitted to employee's Pension Fund Administrator (PFA) monthly
- **Deadline:** 25th of following month

**Example (CEO Sadiq):**
- Gross: ₦60,000
- Employee pension (8%): ₦4,800 (deducted from salary)
- Employer pension (10%): ₦6,000 (company cost)

### 2. Personal Income Tax (PAYE)
- **Regulated By:** Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS)
- **Filing Frequency:** Monthly
- **Deadline:** 21st of following month
- **Tax Bands:** Progressive rates based on annual taxable income
- **Relief:** Standard ₦200,000 annual relief + personal/dependent reliefs
- **Estimate:** Shown in SALARY-STRUCTURE-CODENOVA.md; actual amount varies by individual relief claims

**Responsibility:** HR Manager (Safiya Aminu) calculates, files with FIRS, and provides payslips

### 3. National Health Insurance Scheme (NHIS)
- **Coverage:** Employee + basic family members
- **Payment:** Employer-sponsored (company pays premium)
- **Frequency:** Monthly
- **Provider:** Registered NHIS provider or private insurance

**Current Estimate:** ₦2,500/month per employee

### 4. Other Statutory Requirements
- **Tax Identification Number (TIN):** All employees must register with FIRS
- **National Identification Number (NIN):** Required for PAYE processing
- **Bank Verification Number (BVN):** Linked to NIN for salary disbursement

## Adjustments & Changes

### Salary Increase
1. CEO approves new salary figure
2. Update SALARY-STRUCTURE-CODENOVA.md with new amount
3. Recalculate all affected employee deductions & employer costs
4. Issue adjustment letter to employee
5. Effective date: 1st of next calendar month (typically)
6. Implement in next payroll run
7. Update PAYE calculations with FIRS (notify of change)

### Bonus or Additional Payment
1. CEO authorizes bonus amount & recipient
2. Document in payroll register
3. Add to relevant month's payroll
4. Flag for separate PAYE calculation if applicable
5. Include in payslip with clear labeling

### Deduction from Salary
- Loan deduction, uniform cost, damage reimbursement, etc.
- Require written employee consent (except statutory deductions)
- Document deduction in payroll register
- Show clearly on payslip
